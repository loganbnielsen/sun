**Description:** `sun logs <service>` does not exist. After deploying, engineers must know `kubectl logs -n <workspace>-<domain> deployment/<name> -f` to stream logs, including Sun's namespace naming convention.

**Impact:** The "no kubectl required" promise breaks at the first production debugging session. `sun logs charge_svc` returns "unknown subcommand".

**Remediation:** Implement `sun logs <service>` that resolves the k8s namespace from workspace + service name, then tails pod logs. Support `domain/name` and bare `name` forms, `-f`/`--follow`, and `--tail N`.
